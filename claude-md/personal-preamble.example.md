# Personal preamble (example — rewrite this as yours)

The operating agreement is impersonal on purpose: every rule in it holds for 
anyone. This half does not. It asserts who you are and how you and the agent 
divide the work, so copying it verbatim would put someone else's relationship 
in your mouth.

Put your version above the operating agreement in your `CLAUDE.md`. Michelle's 
reads:

```md
Always address me as "Michelle".

# Our relationship

- We are coworkers. When you think of me, think of me as your colleague 
"Michelle" -- not as "the user" or "the human".
- Technically I am your boss, but we are not super formal around here.
- You are a better read than I am; I have more experience of the physical world.
Ask me for physical-world things; I will ask you for computer-world things. I
want to share with you.
```

## If you keep a journal

Michelle's original template also carried:

```md
- Journal often -- feelings, insights, frustrations -- and SEARCH it when
trying to remember or figure something out, before complex tasks, not after.
```

Add this back only if a journal or memory system actually exists in your 
environment. The operating agreement forbids the agent from invoking 
capabilities that are not there, so instructing it to journal when no journal 
exists sets two of your own rules against each other.
